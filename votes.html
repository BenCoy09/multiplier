Community vote.

Every number on this page is read from $WIF burns on Robinhood Chain and recomputed from the chain, so you can check any vote yourself. Nothing is stored on this server.

Cast your vote

Pick a stock token and an amount. Your vote burns that much $WIF (at least 1) to the dead address. Each whole $WIF is one point toward this week's tally.

$WIF is a memecoin with no promise of value or return. Votes are burned and cannot be undone.

How votes are counted

  • A vote is one burn of whole $WIF to 0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD with the option number added in the smallest unit. For example, 1.000000000000000002 $WIF is option 2.
  • To vote without the button, send exactly one of the amounts listed here.
  • Each whole $WIF burned is one point, so burning 10 $WIF adds 10 points. The token with the most points when the week ends wins. A tie is reported as a tie.
  • A round is one ISO week: Monday 00:00:00 UTC to Sunday 23:59:59 UTC. There is no admin and no schedule job. Each round is recomputed from the chain.
  • Pro passes burn exact whole amounts, so they are never counted as votes. Any other burn to that address shows up under "Other burns".

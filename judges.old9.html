Test it in two minutes

  1. Open the home page. The live radar shows each stock token’s latest multiplier update, read from Robinhood Chain by this server. Replay the SPY update to see what a multiplier change does.
  2. Open the explorer, press Use example wallet, then Analyze. Every figure links to its on-chain transaction, and the page says how the balance was obtained.
  3. Open Rooms. Anyone can read. Posting needs a Pro pass.
  4. To see Pro, burn $WIF as described below, paste the transaction hash under Unlock Pro, and the full holding history, CSV, wallet management, Telegram alerts and posting unlock.

How $WIF is used

  • Pro pass, 30 days: burn $WIF by sending it from your own wallet to the dead address. The server verifies the burn on-chain. The site never connects to a wallet.
  • Watch more wallets: each extra wallet (up to 5 per pass) costs another burn.
  • Renewals: burn again to extend. The Telegram bot reminds you 3 days before a pass ends.
  • Pro unlocks the wallet’s balance at every update, CSV export, Telegram alerts when a multiplier update is scheduled or takes effect, and posting in the rooms.

What is real and what is limited

  • Multiplier history, the live radar and holder balances are read from the chain. If a balance can’t be verified, no figure is shown.
  • Alerts depend on the update event appearing before it takes effect. In the two updates we checked it was about ten minutes early. That is not guaranteed, and updates are rare.
  • A holder badge in rooms means the wallet that paid for the pass holds that token. It does not prove who is typing.
  • Rooms are only as lively as the people in them. Nothing in them is simulated.

ROBINHOOD CHAIN / STOCK TOKENS

Understand what changed in your stock tokens.

Explore multiplier updates on Robinhood Chain and see how they change the underlying shares represented by a wallet’s holdings. Read-only. No wallet connection.

Data is read live from Robinhood Chain through this server. Shares represented = token balance × multiplier. Historical balances are rebuilt from transfer logs up to the last block at or before the update’s effective time. Anyone opening a shared link can see the wallet address in it.
